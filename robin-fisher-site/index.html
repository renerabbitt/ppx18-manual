The approach

Listen first. Solve the room. Refine every detail.

The best design grows out of an organic exchange of ideas. Robin begins with the family, lifestyle and budget, then resolves layout, cabinetry, surfaces, fixtures, lighting and the details that make the finished room feel both intuitive and personal.

  1. 01
    DiscoverLifestyle, priorities, constraints, budget and the way you use the space.
  2. 02
    DesignSpace planning, cabinetry, materials, lighting and a cohesive visual direction.
  3. 03
    CoordinateClear design communication with your contractor, fabricators and trade partners.
